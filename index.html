About Us

A neighborhood sushi tradition

Tucked along Van Buren Boulevard in Riverside, Tomo 7 brings the spirit of a Japanese sushi house to Southern California. Tomo means friend — and that's exactly how we want you to feel the moment you sit down.

Our chefs work with fresh fish and quality ingredients to craft everything from classic nigiri and sashimi to inventive specialty rolls, tempura, teriyaki, ramen, and more. With our all-you-can-eat menu, there's always another favorite to discover.
